# About E-Cell RSCOE

The epicenter of innovation at JSPM's Rajarshi Shahu College of Engineering, built to turn campus thinkers into high-impact venture builders.

"Backed by an active community of 100+ student innovators and leaders, E-Cell RSCOE acts as the launchpad for scalable ventures—providing early mentorship, incubation access, and corporate connections to accelerate ideas into market realities."
