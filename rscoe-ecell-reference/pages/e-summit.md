## What is E-Summit?

E-Summit is E-Cell RSCOE's three-day entrepreneurship experience that brings students, founders, and mentors together for meaningful learning and collaboration.

## Summit Highlights

### Keynote Talks

Insights from founders, operators, and industry leaders building impactful businesses.

### Startup Pitches

Student teams pitch their ideas and products in front of mentors and investors.

### Workshops

Practical sessions on product, growth, branding, and execution for early-stage teams.
